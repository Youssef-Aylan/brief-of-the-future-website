BRIEF OF THE FUTURE

Your privacy.

What happens to what you send us, in plain words.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who runs this

Brief of the Future is run by Youssef Abdelhamid and Sara-jane Collenette.

We're based in New Zealand and work in a global remote team environment. The rules on this page apply wherever we're working.

For anything on this page, write to contact@briefofthefuture.com.

What we collect

Only what you give us:

  • From the contact form or an email: your name, email address, company if you add it, your choices on the form, and what you write.
  • From a booked call: what you enter on Cal.com, and the booking itself.
  • From the newsletter: your email address, handled by Substack.

This site has no analytics and no advertising trackers.

Why we use it

We use it to read your message, reply, book a call if you want one, and keep a record of our conversation. You asked us to get in touch, and that is our reason for using it.

We won't sell it, and we won't add you to a mailing list because you wrote to us.

How AI is involved

AI helps us run this. When you write in, an AI model sorts the message, and another drafts a reply. We read every reply and approve or change it before it goes. The first confirmation is the exception: it's a fixed message with the same words for everyone.

No decision about you is made by AI alone.

Who else handles it

A few services do part of the work, and each sees only what it needs:

  • Vercel runs this website and the form.
  • Resend sends email.
  • reg.ru hosts the contact@ mailbox, and Google's Gmail receives a forwarded copy.
  • Supabase stores the record of our conversation.
  • OpenRouter connects to the AI models.
  • Telegram brings us the alert with your message.
  • Cal.com handles bookings, and Substack handles the newsletter.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the site's fonts and code, so they see your internet address when the page loads.
  • Sentry reports errors in our systems. It's set up to leave personal details out.

How long we keep it

If we've had no contact for 12 months, we erase your name, address and messages from our records. What stays is a count that can't identify you.

Copies can last longer in our email and in backups.

Your choices

Write to contact@briefofthefuture.com at any time to:

  • ask what we hold about you, or for a copy,
  • correct it,
  • have it deleted,
  • tell us to stop contacting you.

We'll do it within 20 working days, and tell you when it's done. If you're not happy with how we've handled it, you can complain to New Zealand's Privacy Commissioner, or to the privacy regulator where you live.

Changes

If this changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
